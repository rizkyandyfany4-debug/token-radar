How the radar prices a token that does not exist yet

implied = round(TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth)

The category multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live-token protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the overall median with a prior of 4 observations, clamped to 0.05–0.60. A protocol is priced like its own category's traded peers — never from a hand-kept list of names. Growth is 1 + the 7-day TVL change (clamped −60% to +120%) × 0.5. Maturity is 0.6 + age/1095 days × 0.4 (age unknown: 365 days). Breadth is 1 + 0.02 per chain beyond the first, at most 5 extra.
